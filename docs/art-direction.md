# Cat Season art direction

## Seed reading

A 256-character random alphanumeric string was generated with `/dev/urandom` and used as inspiration only (it appears nowhere in the game).

- It opens on a triple, `5 3 5`, and keeps returning to doubled letters (`KK`, `44`, `mkmk`). Read as a match-3 rhythm made of repeated small marks, that suggests stitches.
- It is one unbroken run with no separators. That reads as a single woven ground with no cards and no boxes.
- Digits arrive in rare dense bursts (`82074`, `475`, `4892`). Numbers should be the only loud typography.
- Hex-like runs give the palette: `820` (madder brick), `ADB` (pale sage), `475` (deep spruce), `c9` (straw/ochre).
- Mixed case with no pattern means an off-centre, uneven layout with no uniform rhythm.

## 15 shallow directions

1. Woodblock print: the board is a carved block and the tiles are hanko stamps.
2. Knitted sweater: the board is a swatch and the tiles are intarsia motifs.
3. Under the sofa: an extreme low camera with giant furniture. *(unreasonable)*
4. Herbarium page: tiles pressed like specimens with handwritten tags.
5. Split-flap railway board: moves and score flip like departures. *(unreasonable)*
6. Lacquer bento tray: every tile sits in its own compartment.
7. Apartment block at dusk: each tile is a lit window with a cat silhouette.
8. Two-colour risograph zine with misregistered cat stickers.
9. Shrine ema plaques hung on a rope rack.
10. An illuminated medieval bestiary of cats. *(unreasonable)*
11. Veterinary X-ray blueprint. *(unreasonable)*
12. A shop receipt that tallies every treasure as a line item. *(unreasonable)*
13. **Cross-stitch sampler: the whole game is embroidered on linen. Every tile is a stitched motif, the selection is a running stitch, and the HUD is the pattern key.**
14. Leaf pile: the board is buried under leaves that blow off on match.
15. Shadow-puppet theatre: paper cutouts against a lantern screen. *(unreasonable)*

## Pick: 13, the cross-stitch sampler

It turns "match three small marks" into literal X stitches, and code can generate it honestly: every pixel of tile art is a thread.

## Build brief (≤200 words)

**Aesthetic.** A grandmother's autumn sampler. Everything is made of thread on cloth, rendered as real X stitches with over/under strand shading, all painted by `tools/art/generate.py`.

**Palette.** Oatmeal linen `#d8c8a6`, aida white `#efe6d2`, ink brown `#2e2219`, madder brick `#a53f26` (the only loud accent: numbers, primary actions), straw `#c99a2a`, spruce `#2f5a4a`, sage `#8fae86`. Each tile kind gets one thread colour.

**Layout.** Asymmetric. The board is a square of aida basted onto the linen at the left. The HUD sits straight on the linen as a pattern key with no panel. A stitched rule separates its groups. Everything fits in 1280×720 with no scrolling.

**Type.** Fusion Pixel 12px (OFL, CJK), subset to the glyphs the game uses and set at 12px multiples. Titles, buttons and numerals are stitched one font pixel per stitch. Big brick numerals are the only loud type.

**Material.** Linen weave, aida holes, frayed edges and running stitches.

**Forbidden.** Gradients, glows, glass, drop-shadow cards, rounded-card grids, emoji, floating shapes, and the old painted background.

## As built

The sampler sits in a walnut stretcher frame with brass tacks. The key is a framed aida panel, and the buttons are 4-hole sewing buttons. The end card is a handkerchief. Every asset is regenerated by `python3 tools/art/generate.py`. Font licenses are in `src/assets/fonts/fusion-pixel-licenses/`.
